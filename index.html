About KCTV

KCTV is Korey Crawford's personal network — his own channel for his videos, his thoughts and his opinions, broadcasting out of East Lansing, Michigan. No studio, no sponsors calling the shots, no network telling him what he's allowed to say.

Korey is a father of five and a man in recovery who talks about real life the way it actually is — family, money, hard times, second chances, and the lessons that only come the hard way. The pantry and prep videos that built his audience live here too, because they're part of the story.

Everything here is free to watch. If a video hits you, share it with one person who needs to hear it. That's how this network grows.
