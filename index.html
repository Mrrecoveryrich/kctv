About KCTV

KCTV is Korey Crawford's own television network — built and run by him, out of East Lansing, Michigan. No studio, no sponsors calling the shots, no algorithm deciding what matters.

Korey is a father of five, a man in recovery, and a prepper who teaches what he lives: how regular families get ready for blackouts, hard times and whatever comes next — on a real budget, with real talk.

Everything here is free to watch. If an episode helps your family, share it with one person who needs it. That's how this network grows.
